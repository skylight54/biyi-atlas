# 像素皮革与组合键

2026-10-22，网络协议仍为4。

## 使用

第一人称主手或副手持避役地图时，按住潜行键（默认Shift）并右键进入设置。入口跟随原版潜行和使用物品按键绑定，不再注册独立Shift快捷键。单按Shift正常潜行。组合操作在方块/物品使用前消费，并处理Forge主副手两次事件，避免打开设置时同时使用另一件物品。

设置中仍采用双手举图、右侧选项与有界滚轮缩放。Esc、收起按钮或Shift＋右键关闭；单按Shift不关闭。M打开大地图。缩放数据仍存于游戏目录`biyi_atlas/settings.json`。

## 资源

皮革边框由图像生成工具制作，游戏使用的版本随模组源码分发。

正式资源：`src/main/resources/assets/biyi_atlas/textures/atlas/held_leather_frame.png`。1254×1254 RGBA，SHA256 `449aadc6dd1339cad593c35507745470534be93fdf3ce47ac84d8b8da7bb65af`。附带mcmeta显式禁用模糊。原样复制生成结果，没有脚本绘画或修改alpha。中心alpha为0；内部存在极低alpha=1的生成残留，因此没有宣称整个开口逐像素完全透明。旧写实框留在`build/qa/v18/held-leather-v17.png`。

完整提示词：

```text

Generate a NEW low-resolution PIXEL ART thin damaged yellow leather edging around a large empty square hole. Flat top-down game sprite, orthographic, no perspective. Think of a flexible piece of worn tawny ochre hide binding the perimeter of a travel map, NOT a rigid picture frame. Design on a visibly coarse 128x128 pixel grid, crisp large square pixel clusters and a limited 8-color palette, dark brown pixel outlines, honey-yellow/tan highlights, sparse tiny stitch pixels and a few small stepped notches, cracks and frayed corners. Uniform very THIN band only about 5% of canvas width on each side. The central EMPTY opening must cover at least 88% of canvas width AND height. Transparent inside the hole and outside the irregular leather silhouette. The thin band follows all four canvas edges, full square asset filling canvas. Slight uneven torn silhouette but mostly flat flexible hide. No wood, no bevel, no raised carved or embossed molding, no metal, no buckles, no decorative corner ornaments, no rolled scroll ends, no photorealistic fine grain, no blur, no gradients, no lighting shadows, no drop shadow, no map content, no paper, no lettering, no background, no checkerboard drawn into the art. Output only the game-ready edging sprite with real transparent alpha.
